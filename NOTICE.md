# YunqiMC attribution

YunqiMC project owner and maintainer: **YunqiMC**.

New YunqiMC contributions are licensed under GPL-3.0-only unless a source file
explicitly states otherwise. YunqiMC is independently maintained and is not an
official PaperMC distribution.

YunqiMC builds on Paper and its upstream projects. Their original authorship,
copyright notices, and license terms remain applicable. The Paper license and
contributor notices from the pinned upstream are reproduced under
`licenses/upstream/Paper`. The build layout also derives from the
PaperMC/paperweight-examples fork template identified in the README.

The release JAR includes Paperclip and embedded third-party libraries. Their
existing notices are retained inside the JARs; the generated component
inventory lists the included JARs, hashes, and notice filenames without
inferring a license when a notice cannot be identified.
